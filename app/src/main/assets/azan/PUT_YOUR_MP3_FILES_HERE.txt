====================================================================
HOW TO ADD YOUR CUSTOM AZAN AUDIO FILES
====================================================================

Place your two MP3 files directly inside this folder:
  app/src/main/assets/azan/

Required Filenames (case-sensitive, all lowercase):
  1. fajr.mp3   -> Played automatically at Fajr prayer time.
  2. azan.mp3   -> Played automatically at Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha.
====================================================================
